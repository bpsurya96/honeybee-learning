How It All Started 🌱

🇮🇳 HoneyBee Learning is India's first personalised activity book for kids crafting books with each child's name woven into every page.

HoneyBee Learning started in 2024 from a small home studio in Medavakkam, Chennai, with one simple idea — personalised books and return gifts that children actually value.

As a parent, Shanmugi noticed how birthday return gifts were often generic — plastic toys that broke within days, candies that were forgotten, and stationery that never felt special. She wanted something different: gifts that had the child's name on them, that were fun to use, and that parents would genuinely appreciate.

Each book is thoughtfully designed, printed in-house, and packed by hand. Every order is reviewed personally before it ships — because to us, it's not just a product; it's a memory your child will carry.
